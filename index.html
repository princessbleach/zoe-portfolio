Hi, I’m Zoe! I’m a Games Development BSc student at the University for the Creative Arts, graduating in June 2027. My interests sit across game audio, development, and production. I enjoy building soundscapes, designing character voices, implementing audio systems, and experimenting with gameplay ideas.

I also enjoy helping creative teams stay organised and move projects forward. As Project Manager on a team game project, I use Jira and Agile-style sprint planning to support communication, task tracking, and collaboration. I’m building experience across both the creative and organisational sides of game development.
